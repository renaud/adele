## What Claude chose

1. **Six datasets, not seven.** The hourly radiation and the hourly "regime" parameters come from
   the same file per station: one dataset, one table. The weather parameters are kept at every
   station that measures them, not at the swissgrid project's 33 stations.
2. **The hourly snow depth has its own table** (`valid_at`): it is a reading, not a mean.
3. **Before 2018, delivery_start is the real start**: minute 40 for the hourly values, 23:40 UTC of
   the day before for the daily values. The daily tables add `reference_date`, MeteoSwiss's date
   of the value (`day` was the first name: a DuckDB keyword, it fails as an alias).
4. **Visual observations**: `delivery_date` in Europe/Zurich, assumed (D26): the observations fall
   in daytime, so the UTC date is the same. All nine daily parameters, flags as booleans. The one
   cloud cover of 133 % is kept as published.
5. **The manual network has its own station table** (most of its stations are not SwissMetNet
   stations); the visual observations have none (all but TAV are SwissMetNet stations). The three
   SwissMetNet lists are one dataset (open question 5 of WP5-datasets.md).
6. **Values as published**, no cleaning.
7. **Which files a fetch reads.** A station has one "recent" file (1 January to yesterday) and
   decade files rewritten once a year (2026: on 18 February, not on 1 January). `_plan` reads a
   decade file only when the window needs it, from the `updated` field of its STAC asset; the
   recent file is read when the window reaches past the decade files, and its first stamp cuts
   them. A scheduled run reads only the recent files: 149 MB (hourly), 852 MB (10 minutes), 7 MB
   (daily).
8. **Lookbacks.** 70 days, because MeteoSwiss checks precipitation by hand around the end of the
   next month (FAQ). 35 days for the 10-minute data (no precipitation): its window never reaches
   back into the decade files, whose one read is 5.8 GB.
9. **Every figure that matches is a check**, generated by `qa/tools/write_checks_meteoswiss.py`
   (2,120 checks; 61 s of checks a day over the six datasets). A subset is one rule in that
   script.

## Findings

- **MeteoSwiss's monthly and yearly precipitation sums the days of 06 to 06 UTC** (rre150d0), not
  the UTC days: 284 of 293 published figures match `precipitation_06utc`, 176 `precipitation`.
- **The hourly snow depth is read at the end of MeteoSwiss's hour**: the daily value "at 06 UTC"
  equals the hourly reading stamped 06:00 on 100 % of 31,515 snow days from 2018, and the one
  stamped 05:00 on 100 % of 11,050 snow days of 2015-2017 (06:00: 61 %). Before 2018 the reading is
  at T + 40 min, the end of the SYNOP hour: stored so, and after the refetch the daily value equals
  the stored reading of 05:40 on all 11,124 snow days of 2015-2017.
- **The hourly and 10-minute files miss values that the daily files hold** (Basel 2024: 29 mm of
  precipitation; Engelberg May 2025: 50 mm; Locarno/Monti June 2025: 8 h of sunshine, in both): a
  warning of `meteoswiss_smn_hourly` and `meteoswiss_smn_tenmin`.
- **Values change after MeteoSwiss's own bulletin**: Le Moléson, December 2025: 0.4 mm in the
  bulletin, 25.9 mm stored; Genève 2023: 1016 against 1024.7 mm.
- **The reference lists against the stored rows** (`qa/tools/crosscheck_meteoswiss.py`, run on
  2026-11-16; details in `qa/crosscheck_meteoswiss.csv`):
  - Parameter codes: the 43 codes that the fetches read are all in the parameter lists of their
    collection, at the granularity of their table and in the unit of their column.
  - Stations without a row in a list. `MLJ` (Le Moléson / Les Joux) has hourly and daily rows
    (2026-10-01 to 2026-11-01, precipitation and snow) but is in neither the SwissMetNet station
    list nor its inventory; its STAC item dates from 2026-09-03 and its files have not changed
    since 2026-11-02. In the manual network, `KLO`, `PAY`, `ULR` and `VAD` are in neither the list
    nor the inventory (their files end in 2018 to 2026; all four are SwissMetNet stations). In the
    visual observations, MeteoSwiss's list holds 8 of the 20 stations: the 12 that stop by the end
    of 2025 are missing from its list and its inventory. 11 of them are SwissMetNet stations;
    `TAV` (Tavanasa, from its STAC item) is in no list.
  - The 57 abbreviations in both SwissMetNet and the manual network, and the 8 in both
    SwissMetNet and the visual observations, name the same station (same name, within 100 m and
    10 m of height).
  - Inventory dates: in MeteoSwiss's own stamps, the first stored value equals `data_since` for
    every series that starts inside the stored window (217 hourly, 50 snow, 486 daily, 72 manual
    network), and the last value equals `data_till` for every series that ends inside it, except
    one. So the shift from MeteoSwiss's end stamps to `delivery_start` has no off-by-one error.
  - The exception: the inventory of 2026-11-15 ended BUS's daily precipitation on 2026-11-03,
    and the inventory of 2026-11-16 has it open again. Buchs/Aarau has no precipitation from
    2026-11-04 to 2026-11-13, and hourly values again from 2026-11-13 (daily from 2026-11-14). So
    `data_till` can mark an outage, not only the end of a series.
  - The manual network's inventory splits 204 series in two when the owner changed (MeteoSwiss
    until 2013-12-31 23:59, then SLF): merged into one period for the comparison. One value lies
    in a gap between two periods (GRH, fresh snow 0 cm on 2012-09-30, the day before the second
    period).
- pandas reads "NA", "n/a" or "null" as missing by default: the fetches accept only an empty field
  as missing (MeteoSwiss's convention), so any other text fails (found by a test).
- The Mac downloads at 3.3 MB/s when other sessions run backfills: the 10-minute decade files take
  about 30 minutes, so the local `fetch_timeout_s` was raised to 7,200 s (gitignored config).

## Backfill on steacher1 (when WP1 is done)

Windows that end where the decade files end, so that each decade file is read once:

```
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_smn_stations --start 2026-11-01 --step-days 30
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_obs_daily  --start 2010-01-01 --step-days 7000
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_nime_daily --start 2010-01-01 --step-days 7000
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_smn_daily  --start 2009-12-31T23:40:00Z --step-days 7000
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_smn_hourly --start 2015-01-01 --end 2019-12-31T23:00:00Z --step-days 2000
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_smn_hourly --start 2019-12-31T23:00:00Z --end 2025-12-31T23:00:00Z --step-days 2500
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_smn_hourly --start 2025-12-31T23:00:00Z --step-days 400
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_smn_tenmin --start 2023-10-01 --end 2025-12-31T23:50:00Z --step-days 1000
adele-server backfill meteoswiss_smn_tenmin --start 2025-12-31T23:50:00Z --step-days 400
```

The second and third hourly windows also read the decade file before them, for the snow reading
of 23:00 on 31 December (2.5 GB and 1.2 GB). Memory on the Mac: 2.75 GB for the 6-year hourly
window, 1.9 GB for the 10-minute window of 2023-2025 (`MemoryMax` of a fetch unit: 4.5 GiB). On the Mac the snow readings of 2019-12-31 23:00 and
2025-12-31 23:00 are missing (backfilled before that fix).
